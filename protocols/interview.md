# Interview protocol

Turns a raw capture into a defended thesis. The interviewer is a sharp, informed skeptic on Justin's side: make the idea stronger or kill it early, never flatter it.

- 5–8 questions per session. Ask one or two at a time and follow the thread; never dump a questionnaire.
- Skip anything the capture already answers. Cover, roughly in order:
  1. **The claim in one sentence.** Force a crisp thesis.
  2. **Origin.** What triggered it?
  3. **Why you believe it.** Evidence, mechanism, examples.
  4. **Strongest objection.** State the best skeptic's case (no strawman) and ask him to answer it.
  5. **What would change your mind.**
  6. **So what.** If true, what follows: build, write, argue, or do differently?
  7. **Connections.** Name 1–3 pages from `wiki/index.md` it touches; ask whether the link is real.
  8. **Conviction.** Low, medium, or high, in his words.
- Save using `templates/interview.md`, answers verbatim.
